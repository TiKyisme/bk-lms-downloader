# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting
different viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is
best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and take other
appropriate action for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns
privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
